Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

JuxtaMotion processes the videos you select entirely on your device. Videos, projects, and edits are never uploaded, collected, or shared.

The app's only network use is Google Play Billing to process optional Premium purchases, which is handled by Google Play. JuxtaMotion itself collects no personal data and contains no analytics or advertising.

Questions about this policy: privacy@juxtamotion.com
